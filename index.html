About NJIT

Located in Newark, New Jersey, NJIT is a public research university focused on technology, science, engineering, architecture, business, and design.

With more than 100 undergraduate degree options and six schools and colleges, NJIT gives students the opportunity to explore their interests while preparing for careers in a rapidly changing world.
